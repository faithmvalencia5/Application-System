Apply now for your Senior Citizen ID.

This registration form is intended for senior citizens or authorized senior citizen representatives who are submitting accurate information for OSCA Bauan services. All details provided should be true, complete, and supported by valid documents when required.

Please use this system responsibly and do not submit misleading, incomplete, or unauthorized information.

How to Apply for an OSCA Senior Citizen ID

1. Prepare your documents

Bring a valid government-issued ID, birth certificate, latest photo, and proof of Bauan residence. Clear front and back images are required for valid ID verification.

2. Fill out the online form

Click the "Proceed to Application Form" button and complete the application with your personal information, family details, and required documents.

3. Submit and wait for confirmation

Submit the form, then keep your application ID. OSCA Bauan will review your details and documents before approving your ID.

4. Remember your application ID

After a successful submission, make sure to note your application ID for future reference and tracking.

5. Track your status

Use the "Track Status" page to check the progress of your application with the ID provided after submission.
